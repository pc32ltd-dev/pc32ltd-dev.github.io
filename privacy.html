PC32 Stock Checker – Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy: PC32 Stock Checker Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who this applies to

PC32 Stock Checker is a private tool run by [your name or business name] for managing the stock of one business. It is used only by its owner, using the owner's own Google account.

What the tool accesses

Gmail: it reads emails the owner has labelled "Deliveries" (order, dispatch, delivery and invoice emails from suppliers), including attached PDF invoices. It also adds its own labels to those emails ("Processed" and "Check-manually"). Google Sheets: it reads and writes a spreadsheet owned by the same account, which holds the delivery records, product list and logs. Google Apps Script services: it runs on a timer and stores a small amount of settings data (such as the time of the last run).

Who else receives data

Google Gemini: the text of supplier emails and the contents of supplier PDF invoices are sent to Google's Gemini service to read the product lines. No personal details are needed for this, but the emails may contain business addresses and order details. Square: once a delivery has been confirmed, stock quantities for the matching products are sent to the business's own Square account. Customer information is never sent.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or publish data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models. Data is not sent to anyone other than the services listed above. It stays in the owner's Google account and Square account.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept in the owner's spreadsheet for as long as the owner wants them. The owner can delete any data at any time by deleting rows or the spreadsheet, and can remove the tool's access to their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
