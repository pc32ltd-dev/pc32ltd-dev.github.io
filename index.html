PC32 Stock Checker

A private tool used by one small business to keep its drinks stock records up to date. It reads delivery and order emails from the owner's own Gmail account, lists the items in the owner's own Google Sheet, and, once a person has counted and confirmed the delivery, updates stock levels in the business's Square account. This tool is for personal business use by its owner and is not offered to the public. Contact: pc32ltd@gmail.com
